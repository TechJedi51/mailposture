Guide and glossary

Help

MailPosture turns mail-security records and reports into a prioritized, read-only status view.

Getting started

  1. Add domains.Open Settings and add every domain that appears after the @ in your organization’s From addresses.
  2. Add selectors and endpoints.Edit each domain to add the DKIM selectors used by its senders and the TLS services whose certificates should be monitored.
  3. Review the dashboard.Start with the master score and attention list, then open a domain to inspect evidence and the next recommended action.
  4. Resolve and recheck.Make DNS or service changes outside MailPosture, allow them to propagate, then select Run checks.
Checks

What MailPosture evaluates

DMARC

Checks whether the domain publishes one valid DMARC policy, its enforcement level, coverage percentage, and aggregate-report destination.

DMARC aggregate reports

Shows message volume, DMARC, DKIM, and SPF alignment, trends, and the top failing sources without exposing message content.

DMARC failure reports

Shows the number of individual failure reports. MailPosture intentionally does not display message samples because they can contain personal or confidential content.

DKIM

Checks each configured selector for a published public key and reports missing, revoked, or weak RSA keys. Selectors must be entered because DNS cannot reliably discover them.

MTA-STS

Checks the DNS signal, HTTPS policy file, policy mode, and whether the policy covers every published MX host.

TLS reporting

Checks for a valid TLS-RPT record and summarizes SMTP TLS successes, failures, reporting organizations, and failure types from parsedmarc. Processed TLS-RPT messages normally move to Archive/SMTP-TLS; MailPosture reads the normalized OpenSearch data.

TLS certificates

Connects to configured direct-TLS endpoints, validates trust, and reports days until expiration. SMTP STARTTLS on ports 25 and 587 is not currently tested.

BIMI

Checks the BIMI record, DMARC prerequisites, HTTPS logo, safe SVG content, and whether a mark certificate URL is published.

Terms

Glossary

Alignment
The authenticated SPF or DKIM domain matches the domain visible in the message’s From address according to DMARC rules.
Selector
The label identifying a DKIM public key, such as selector1._domainkey.example.com.
Policy
DMARC uses none, quarantine, or reject. MTA-STS uses none, testing, or enforce.
RUA
The address that receives aggregate DMARC or TLS reports.
MX
A DNS record identifying the servers that receive email for a domain.
TLS endpoint
A host and port that presents a certificate directly, such as mail.example.com:465.
Posture score
A summary indicator weighted by control status. It helps prioritize work but does not replace the evidence shown for each check.

What MailPosture changes

MailPosture reads public DNS, HTTPS and TLS endpoints, and OpenSearch report data. In the standalone deployment, saving Settings writes parsedmarc’s active configuration, triggers an automatic parsedmarc reload, and manages the OpenSearch snapshot policy. parsedmarc—not MailPosture—moves report messages into its Archive subfolders.
